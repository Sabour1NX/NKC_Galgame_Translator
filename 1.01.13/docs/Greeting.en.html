Playing an untranslated game

When your language is missing

  • A niche release may never get an official translation.
  • Separate subtitles pull your eyes away from the dialogue and choices.
  • Finding a fan patch takes time, and some download sites bundle unwanted programs.
  • Even a game you bought may have no option for your language.
