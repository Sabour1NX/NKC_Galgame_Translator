All real-API observations and limitations

GPU controls and their limits

Quality 0–100

Default 75 changes the detector resolution cap, quantized to 32 pixels. Small images can be equivalent across settings. Higher values do not guarantee higher accuracy.

1024MiB arena budget

Range 256–16384MiB. This budgets detector/recognizer arenas, not all process, driver or library allocations. It neither reserves nor forces a full 1 GiB allocation.

Auto gives classic English pixel frames CPU priority; manual Windows/Pixel modes stay on CPU. Model confidence 95 is not a measured 95% accuracy promise. Missing/incompatible components have explicit CPU fallback.
